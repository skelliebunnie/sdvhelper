# Goal
My ultimate goal for this isn't to be a replacement of the wiki, but to address certain things that I struggle with. For example, I actually know when and where a lot of fish spawn and their weather conditions, but I find it hard to plan a fishing day because there's a lot of fish available and keeping it all straight in my head is hard. So I'd like to see a "day schedule" type layout with which fish are available given the season and weather conditions. Is it raining in spring? Great - what fish are available, per location, at what times?

I also want to see what the shortest viable list of loved gifts I can carry around is - especially when I take that Qi Gift quest to give 50 loved gifts in one week. Sure, I have a pretty good idea of what everyone loves, but do you want to run around with 20 items when you could get away with 8? I don't - I want to have my stuff for a mining run on me after I shove gifts in everyone's face!

2. Item Detail page
Continuing the trend of not working on the fish schedule right away, I'd like to create the item detail template where info like fish behavior and difficulty will be listed. This way I can keep the fish schedule very simple - name, location, times.

3. Finally, the Fish Schedule
And then the actual Fish Schedule! This will display a list of fish filtered by weather in a "time table"/schedule layout, organized by location. I'd like to be able to toggle locations on/off, and of course switch weather conditions. Visually it should look like a "day planner" layout.
